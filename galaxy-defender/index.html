❔ HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD / Arrow keys, or the left virtual joystick on touch.

Fire: Automatic (Auto-fire). Hold Space to shoot if Auto-fire is off.

Abilities: Keys 1 / 2 / 3 or the on-screen buttons — EMP stuns, Nova Bomb deals huge damage, Time Dilation slows enemies.

Pause: P or Esc. Mute: M.

Power-ups: Rapid Fire · Damage ×2 · Repair +30 HP · Shield +40 · Speed Boost.

Goal: Clear waves, beat bosses, earn credits, upgrade your ship. You have 3 lives per run. HP 0 loses a life.

Tip: Shields absorb damage first. Save Nova Bomb for bosses.
