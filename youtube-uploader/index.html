YouTube Shorts Uploader

YouTube Shorts Uploader is a private automation tool owned and operated by Shahin Ahmed Rafi.

It creates original AI-generated Bangla cartoon stories for children — including voiceover, visuals, and subtitles — and uploads them as YouTube Shorts to the owner's own channel, GolpoJadu Bangla, on a daily schedule.

This is not a public service. It is used solely by the owner to manage his own YouTube channel. No other users, accounts, or channels are involved.

The app accesses the owner's YouTube channel through Google OAuth (upload videos, read channel info and analytics) strictly to publish the owner's own content.
