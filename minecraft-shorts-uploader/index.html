Minecraft Shorts Uploader

Minecraft Shorts Uploader is a private automation tool owned and operated solely by Shahin Ahmed Rafi. It creates original AI-generated Minecraft-themed YouTube Shorts and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. Story ideas, scripts, and scene prompts are prepared for each Short.
  2. Video clips are generated with AI video tools, then edited, captioned, and combined into a finished vertical video.
  3. A thumbnail, title, description, and hashtags are prepared for each video.
  4. With the owner's authorization, the finished video is uploaded to the owner's YouTube channel via the official YouTube Data API.

Who is it for?

Nobody but the owner. This app has no public users, no sign-up page, and no public functionality. It exists only to automate publishing to one YouTube channel owned by Shahin Ahmed Rafi.
