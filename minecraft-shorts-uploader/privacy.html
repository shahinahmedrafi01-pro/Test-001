Privacy Policy — Minecraft Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What this app does

Minecraft Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner, Shahin Ahmed Rafi, to create and publish original AI-generated Minecraft-themed YouTube Shorts to his own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, and only with the owner's explicit consent, the app may:

  • Upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel
  • Read the channel's basic info, video list, and analytics (views, watch time)

How data is used

All accessed data is used solely to publish and monitor videos on the owner's own channel. Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's automation server and are never shared.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. There are no advertisers, analytics providers, or other integrations.

Children's privacy

The app itself does not collect personal data from children or any other users — it has no public users at all. Videos published by the app are marked with the correct YouTube audience setting based on each video's actual content, following YouTube's policies.

Data retention & deletion

OAuth tokens remain stored only while the automation is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time via Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com > Security > Third-party access), which immediately stops all data access.
